# EXP02 — Temperature Response

## Objective
Measure thermal response to a controlled **manual/external** change. The baseline does not claim autonomous heater control.

## Record
- starting temperature;
- intervention time and method;
- temperature at fixed intervals;
- time to peak/target;
- recovery/stabilization time;
- pH during the response as contextual data.

## Stop condition
Stop if temperature leaves the teacher-approved safe experimental range or any equipment behaves unexpectedly.
