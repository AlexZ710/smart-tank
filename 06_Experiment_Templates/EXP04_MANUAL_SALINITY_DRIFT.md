# EXP04 — Manual Salinity Drift

## Measurement boundary
There is no EC/conductivity sensor. Salinity is measured manually with a refractometer/hydrometer.

## Record
- starting water mass/level reference;
- manual salinity SG at each checkpoint;
- temperature and pH telemetry;
- evaporation/top-off intervention time and amount;
- salinity after recovery.

Never derive or label an automated EC value.
