# EXP05 — Organic-Load Risk Observation

## Purpose
Observe risk indicators after a teacher-approved organic-load simulation.

## Available evidence
- pH trend;
- temperature;
- PT550 operational light trend;
- manual water-clarity notes;
- optional manual ammonia reagent reading.

## Interpretation boundary
Do not equate falling pH with measured ammonia. If ammonia was not tested manually, report it as unmeasured.
